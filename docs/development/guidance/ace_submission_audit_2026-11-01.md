## Scientific case and limits

The strongest independent-system evidence is the frozen prospective study:20 systems in each of two generator families, two histories averaged within each system, fixed deployment initialization, reserved joint-action blocks, four tests with Holm adjustment. Delivery/control geometric NMSE ratios are:

- Five-node rolling:2.45956, marginal95% interval1.78782–3.38368; the effect favors rolling.
- Five-node selected flat refit:0.356194, interval0.282389–0.449288; registered superiority met.
- Thirty-node rolling:0.274321, interval0.158409–0.47505; registered superiority met.
- Thirty-node selected flat refit:0.391027, interval0.254548–0.600681; registered superiority met.

These are ratios of paired errors, not intervention counts or comparisons to random acquisition. Supervision, parameterization and computation differ between SCM and flat. The primary rule is a point estimate at most0.8, an upper marginal interval below1 and adjusted p below0.05; it is not95% assurance of a20% population gain.

Stage A's480 fits provide an exploratory recipe comparison on an exposed grid. The confirmation covers12 histories of one emulator, using the median of three scored refit initializations. Neither supplies twelve independent physical systems or a deployable initialization-selection rule. The physical study retains11 conditions: delivery beats rolling in7, physics in3 and Fourier in0. It is one apparatus, not broad physical validation.

The theory distinguishes eligible observations, measured-parent fitting, predicted-parent deployment, joint support and snapping error. Its assumptions are explicit; it does not certify fitted neural predictors or identify the cause of the family reversal. The practical contribution is an evaluation discipline: compare the online and delivered predictors, retain simple controls, and evaluate the composed chain under the intended intervention support.

Independent scientific audit found no result-invalidating contradiction or mandatory new experiment for that scope. The principal review risks are audience interest beyond established scratch refitting, limited generator/baseline breadth, and the practical meaning of relative improvements when absolute errors are already small. Stronger controls, noise/internal-action studies and multiple apparatuses would support broader claims; they are not repairs that justify changing the frozen studies.
