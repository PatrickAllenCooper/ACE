# Review of the proposed ACE experiment design

November 14, 2026. Independent agent Helmholtz reviewed the November 12 experimental agenda against the completed 24-system results. This was a new, read-only critique of E1, E5 and E8. It did not repeat completed scientific checks or execute models, experiments, SSH commands or installations. It is not a human review or acceptance of a runnable protocol.

## Findings and handoff dispositions

1. **Align endpoint and admission units.** E1 could choose threshold-band cost or fixed-budget log error, while E5 treated its threshold as an error percentage and converted it to savings. Cost from an averaged curve also differs from expected crossing cost or spending. The November 14 handoff requires a defined estimand, consistent units and measured budget–error curves or public stopping for savings claims. Source: [agenda line 157](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:157), [line 198](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:198) and [line 253](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:253).

2. **Validate the complete analysis.** Average calibration can conceal policy-specific biases. Mixing oracle and uninformed actions at a known rate does not generally create the same fraction of improvement under adaptive trajectories or nonlinear endpoints. The handoff requires paired-effect, coverage and false-positive checks, plus independently established effects for synthetic controls. Source: [agenda line 146](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:146) and [line 265](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:265).

3. **Do not call a heuristic reference a ceiling.** The proposal acknowledges that Bayes-optimal sequential planning was not computed. The handoff calls the oracle-informed heuristic an informed reference and requires matched algorithms, computation and learner interfaces to isolate information value. Exceeding that reference can motivate investigation but does not prove leakage. Source: [agenda line 49](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:49), [line 240](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:240) and [line 363](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:363).

4. **Reserve untouched confirmation text.** E5 uses held-out language families for class admission, while E8 does not clearly reserve different families for confirmation. The handoff separates admission, tuning and confirmation texts before admission, including author or template separation. Source: [agenda line 254](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:254) and [line 337](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:337).

5. **Power both required confirmation contrasts.** Half the informed-reference gap does not determine the effect against an equal-information comparator. The handoff requires a meaningful effect for each primary contrast and power for the joint decision rule, including systems, texts, training seeds and replicates. The proposed 96 to 156 systems remain provisional. Source: [agenda line 258](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:258) and [line 356](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:356).

6. **Match claims to contrasts.** H1 alone cannot show that text caused a gain, and H1 plus H2 cannot establish feedback use or recovery from misleading text. The handoff limits primary conclusions to named frozen comparators and requires separate prespecified ablations for behavioral claims. Transfer remains a separate result. Source: [agenda line 352](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:352) and [line 428](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/ace_llm_experimental_agenda_2026-11-12.md:428).

## Meaning of this disposition

All six findings are incorporated as requirements in the [November 14 handoff](/Users/pat/code/ACE/docs/development/guidance/handoff_ace_llm_research_2026-11-14.md). The original agenda remains preserved. The findings are not resolved in executable study code, because no such successor protocol has been implemented or qualified here.

A minimum convincing study validates one coherent endpoint and its complete analysis, then confirms a frozen recipe against prespecified strong controls on untouched systems and text families. It needs enough independent training seeds and joint power for its required contrasts. Experimental savings and behavioral claims need their own evidence from reliable public stopping and matched ablations.
