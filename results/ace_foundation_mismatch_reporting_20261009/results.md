## What changed in the evidence

The first component pilot favored a numerical grammar that contained every true mechanism family. This new, separately frozen screen deliberately removed the true sine family from that grammar in two of four variants. With a sine replacement at M, TabPFN24's composed-Y geometric paired NMSE ratio to Grammar32 is **0.015875**; with the replacement at Y it is **0.014407**. TabPFN wins6/6systems in each of these two strata. Those ratios correspond to about98.4% and98.6% lower geometric paired error, respectively. Arithmetic means of the paired ratios are0.018799 and0.017431, also strongly favorable.

This establishes a useful conditional result for these fixed pipelines: a pretrained numerical alternative can overcome a deliberately inadequate compact mechanism grammar, even using fewer labels for fitting. It does not establish a unique benefit of pretraining over all flexible non-pretrained regressors, general transfer to real systems, causal graph discovery, or fewer interventions. Both learners use the supplied SCM graph, and this pilot holds histories fixed.

## Composition gains do not imply preservation of each mechanism

On fixed local-Y probes spanning M∈[−2,2], PFN24 worsens the unchanged Y mechanism relative to its retained pre-change grammar on all6systems in each of the null, coefficient-M-change and missing-M-family strata. The mean increases in absolute local-Y MSE are0.148352,0.126522 and0.130673, respectively. These probes include extrapolation beyond the training intervention menu; they do not establish equal harm on every reachable root-action distribution.

The mechanism mixture likewise worsens unchanged Y in6/6null systems and5/6coefficient-change systems, with mean increases0.067208 and0.082304. The terminal mixture in the missing-M stratum selects100%PFN on all6systems and therefore inherits its unchanged-Y diagnostic harm despite excellent composed performance. Its diagnostic blended heads are not a mechanistic decomposition of its complete-model forecast.

For missing-Y variants, the unchanged M mechanism worsens under PFN24 in3/6systems, mean local-M MSE increase0.000274612. The effect is not a universal catastrophe, but preservation is demonstrably not guaranteed. All local harm differences, including improvements and null-case refitting harm for grammar baselines, remain in the complete report.

The result supports the earlier theory distinction: selecting good terminal predictions does not identify correct individual mechanisms or supply a no-harm guarantee. The present calibration objective uses noisy Y, while the private target disables disturbances. With nonlinear Y, their conditional means can differ. Five-row surrogate calibration and different5-versus25candidate flexibility limit attribution of mixture differences; neither mixture is declared universally superior.

## Recommended next method and experiment

Keep numerical grammar as an explicit option. Develop **retention-aware selection per mechanism**: candidate heads include the retained pre-change predictor, a newly fitted numerical predictor and a pretrained alternative. Selection should consider both eligible measured-parent validation and the composed target, with an explicit penalty or abstention rule for damage to an unchanged mechanism. These are proposed criteria, not proven safeguards. Training-only support checks should distinguish interpolation from extrapolation; no private probe can become a tuning input.

Before making an acquisition-efficiency claim, add a fixed flexible non-pretrained regression baseline to a new protocol. The present deliberately misspecified grammar comparison cannot isolate pretraining from representational flexibility. Freeze that learner and all tuning/splitting rules on fresh seeds before scoring; do not introduce a sine-specific rescue into the completed screen.

Only after those methods are specified should a separate crossed estimator×acquisition experiment compare random, coverage and uncertainty-based intervention selection. Prespecify equal legal menus, charged-response budgets, untouched evaluation, failure treatment, stopping thresholds and inference costs. Report a learning curve or interventions-to-target directly. This pilot measured neither quantity. No further run is launched by this recommendation.
