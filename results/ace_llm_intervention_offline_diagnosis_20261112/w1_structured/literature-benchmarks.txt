# literature-benchmarks
## SUMMARY
Read-only web research on 2026-11-12; nothing was installed, run or signed up for.

The main finding is that the local Causal Chambers assessment is outdated. There is now a self-service Remote Lab with a real-time Python API to physical wind and light tunnels, billed per second, and the pip package
  ships MIT-licensed mechanistic simulators. It is the only candidate I found that combines a validated causal graph, real do-operations, a monetary cost and prose variable descriptions.

Ranked shortlist for the concrete experimentalist task (effort figures are my estimates; no tool was prototyped):
1. Causal Chambers wind tunnel: simulator-backed development (about 1 week), then frozen policies on real hardware (1 to 2 weeks plus human sign-up and budget). Claim scope is one apparatus.
2. Compressor rig: pyCycle (pure Python, 1 to 2 weeks) or ThermoPower via OpenModelica and FMPy (2 to 3 weeks). Simulation only, synthetic costs.
3. BoxingGym location finding, temporal discounting and death process (3 to 5 days each; adapters partly exist). No costs, not do-operations.
4. bnlearn networks with supplied graphs (Gaussian nets 3 to 5 days; discrete nets 1 to 2 weeks). No natural cost, memorization risk.
5. A PhysGym-style prior ladder applied as a control inside options 1 to 4.

Gene-perturbation pools, SERGIO and Tennessee Eastman are poor fits or unverified. CLeaR 2027 closes in four days; ICML 2027 is the next announced deadline.

## KEY FACTS
[1] (verified) Causal Chambers Remote Lab gives exclusive real-time sessions (set, measure, wait, batches) on physical Wind Tunnel Mk2 and Light Tunnel Mk2 via a sign-up form. One credit is one second of runtime;
  academic rate 0.014 EUR per credit; 1000 free credits; minimum session 60 s.
    EVIDENCE: https://docs.causalchamber.ai/remote-lab/quickstart ; /running-a-real-time-experiment ; /how-credits-work ; http://www.causalchamber.ai/
[2] (verified) The pip package causalchamber (MIT; 0.2.8; pre-release 1.0.0rc1 of 2026-10-29 needs Python 3.11+) includes numpy-only wind-tunnel simulators: ModelA1/A2 (fan speed), ModelB1 (current),
  SimA1C2/SimA1C3/SimA2C3 (noisy downwind pressure from load_in, load_out, hatch). It is not installed locally.
    EVIDENCE: https://pypi.org/project/causalchamber/ ; raw causalchamber/simulators/wt/main.py and simulators/README.md ; CHANGES.txt ; local import check with /usr/bin/python3
[3] (verified) The chamber physics is documented with stated misspecification: affinity-law speed, cubic current, squared-speed pressure, linear hatch term, fans modelled independently although coupled. Graph edges were
  validated by randomized experiments with KS tests (one wind-tunnel edge p = 0.082).
    EVIDENCE: arXiv 2404.11341v2, Appendix IV and V

    EVIDENCE: https://box.causalchamber.ai/gtc.pdf, all 10 pages read
[5] (verified) ThermoPower 4.0.0 (Modelica License 2) has IdealGas.Compressor with beta-line map tables. TestCompressorConstSpeed, TestCompressorInertia and both BraytonCycle simulators ran in the OpenModelica master

    EVIDENCE: https://build.openmodelica.org/Documentation/ThermoPower.IdealGas.Compressor.html ; https://libraries.openmodelica.org/branches/master/ThermoPower/ThermoPower.html ;

[6] (likely) pyCycle is Apache-2.0 (pip om-pycycle, 4.2.0) and ships compressor map files (axi5, HPC_map, LPC_map, Fan_map, ncp01). TESPy is MIT but its map formulation has no rotational-speed parameter. Neither was run,

    EVIDENCE: https://github.com/OpenMDAO/pyCycle (LICENSE.txt, pycycle/maps) ; https://tespy.readthedocs.io/en/main/api/tespy.components.turbomachinery.html
[7] (verified) BoxingGym: MIT, 10 environments, prior versus no-prior variants, 5 runs with standard errors, no tests, no cost model. Priors do not reliably help. Three environments match the Deep Adaptive Design tasks

    EVIDENCE: https://github.com/kanishkg/boxing-gym ; arXiv 2501.01540v2 ; ar5iv 2103.02438
[8] (verified) Whether LLMs use experimental feedback is contested. Gupta et al. 2025: permuted feedback left performance unchanged and classical methods usually won. arXiv 2603.26177: feedback gives +53.4% discoveries

    EVIDENCE: arXiv 2509.21403v1 ; arXiv 2603.26177 ; arXiv 2507.15550v2
[9] (verified) Comparable papers use 3 to 10 environments or tens of tasks, 5 to 10 seeds, fixed-budget endpoints, SD or SE, and no formal tests (DAD, LLAMBO, BoxingGym, BioDiscoveryAgent, LeGIT, PhysGym). Cost-to-target
  with censoring is rare.
    EVIDENCE: ar5iv 2103.02438 ; arXiv 2402.03921v2 ; 2501.01540v2 ; 2405.17631v3 ; 2503.01139v2 ; 2507.15550v2
[10] (verified) No pip-installable, license-clear Tennessee Eastman simulator with on-demand interventions was verified. CausalBench and GeneDisco are logged pools without a supplied graph. SERGIO requires numeric gene

## IMPLICATIONS FOR AGENDA
- Propose the wind tunnel as the concrete experimentalist task in two stages: a simulator-backed development family now, with no external service, then a real-chamber run of frozen policies. Stage two needs human sign-
  up, terms acceptance and a budget, and its claim is limited to one apparatus.
- Do not make ThermoPower the first engineering step. Prototype pyCycle first, or budget for an OpenModelica container plus FMU qualification. Replace the agenda's dead ThermoPower link.
- Build three controls into every LLM comparison, because informed reviewers will expect them: permuted feedback, a prior ladder (full text, no context, anonymized names), and a numerical policy given the same semantic
  features.
- Add a larger frozen-model arm before concluding anything about the LLM route. The literature suggests feedback use depends on model capability, which matters for a 0.6B policy (my inference).
- Get independent-system statistics from many simulated systems (chamber-simulator parameter draws, compressor maps, bnlearn Gaussian networks). Treat the real chamber as a case study, not a population.
- Keep capped cost-to-target, and add fixed-budget curves, interquartile means with stratified bootstrap intervals and performance profiles so results are comparable with the literature.
- Do not spend effort on gene-perturbation pools, SERGIO or Tennessee Eastman as primary environments.
- Plan around ICML 2027 only for a narrow paper. UAI 2027 (estimated February) or NeurIPS 2027 (estimated May) fit the full agenda; TMLR is rolling with a journal-to-conference route; ICLR 2027 workshops (suggested 1 Feb
  2027) suit early feedback.
