# literature-policy
## SUMMARY
All ten works cited in the agenda exist and are described accurately. The agenda misses the closest prior art. Trained LLM information-gathering policies now include Paprika (SFT then SFT+DPO, ICML 2025), ASIG, and Kriz
  et al. (arXiv, Oct 2026), whose mechanism check trains the same base model, Qwen3-0.6B, to a state-dependent selection policy with on-policy GRPO. Numerical amortized intervention policies (CAASL, NeurIPS 2024. GO-
  CBED, TMLR 09/2026) already cover goal-oriented causal design and claim transfer. The careful ablations (Gupta, Kristiadi, BoxingGym, EVOLvE, Krishnamurthy) agree: LLMs supply priors and proposals, rarely adaptation to
  measurements, and LLM students distilled from numerical teachers trail them. The constant action has eight documented candidate causes, most separable offline on saved artifacts. The completed screen (known graph,
  linear, known noise, eight actions, anonymous variables) is the regime where the literature predicts no LLM advantage. What stays open is narrow: a trained SCM-conditioned LLM policy that passes feedback-shuffling
  controls and beats goal-oriented numerical planners on cost to target where the planner is handicapped for a reason declared in advance. Coverage is about 130 searches and fetches, not a systematic review.

## SURPRISES / CONTRADICTIONS
- The public preprint and the README claim a large DPO benefit that the audited 24-system screen does not reproduce. The guidance documents do not mention the preprint identifier.
- The learned numerical ranker collapsed too, onto two X interventions. The handoff and report discuss collapse only for the LLM arms.
- The same base model, Qwen3-0.6B with LoRA, learned a state-dependent policy in a concurrent paper using on-policy RL on a categorical state.
- The best-evidenced remedy, giving the LLM planner statistics, reduces it to addition and argmax, which conflicts with the requirement that the LLM contribute to the decision.
- BoxingGym reports that prior information does not improve performance and that adding an explicit statistical model does not reliably help. The agenda cites it only as a benchmark.
- One on-topic 2026 preprint (arXiv:2606.31184) was withdrawn by its author. LeGIT's abstract mentions benchmark scales that the body does not show. Several items are flagged unverified in notes section 10.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR AGENDA
- Compute a headroom table on existing systems before any training: random, coverage, best cost-constrained static Bayesian design, myopic target-risk, a non-myopic reference. If static matches the planner, redesign the
  testbed (unknown noise, nonlinearity, misspecification, semantics, larger menus) first.
- Run offline discriminating diagnostics on saved artifacts: prior-only loss baseline, chosen/rejected action tabulation over exposed pairs, full-command normalized scoring versus greedy constrained decoding, identifier
  and menu permutation, copy-previous-action predictor, linear probe for planner scores.
- Diagnose the numerical ranker alongside the LLM. Any supervision fix (graded listwise targets, balancing, more distinct states, DAgger relabeling) should be shown to un-collapse the numerical student first.
- Do not scale offline DPO. If a feedback stage is proposed, prefer on-policy RL with entropy pressure and permuted identifiers, mirroring the published Qwen3-0.6B result, with reasoning tokens and a predeclared capacity
  ladder as separate arms.
- Test input redesign as two separate arms: summarized SCM statistics, and per-action planner statistics. The second needs an argmax-of-prompt baseline, because it risks the narrator role the handoff excludes.
- Add comparators: a GO-CBED-style goal-oriented amortized numerical policy, a prediction-oriented information criterion, a semantic-feature numerical baseline on LLM embeddings, and LLM hybrids (warm start then planner,
  acquisition selection).
- Add controls: feedback shuffling on policy input, state-blind and state-permuted adapters, anonymous and misleading metadata on freshly generated systems, training-seed replication, censored-cost analysis with
  threshold sensitivity.
- Move the LLM to a role the evidence supports (semantic priors with recovery, language-only objectives or constraints, mechanism proposals under misspecification, large action spaces) and declare the planner's handicap
  in advance.
- Update related work and drop the novelty hedge on SFT-then-preference. Reconcile or supersede the public preprint and README claim before any submission.

## OPEN QUESTIONS
- How do 3,584 retained comparisons map to 119 preference updates, and is one action chosen far more often than rejected in the exposed pairs?
- Does the best static design already match target-risk planning on the 24 systems, given ten met the target after initialization?
- What are the token lengths of the eight commands and the menu order shown in the prompt, and does greedy constrained decoding pick the highest-probability full command?
- Were intermediate adapters saved, so learning curves of state sensitivity can be drawn without new training?
- Is Qwen3 digit-level tokenization confirmed? I found one secondary source for Qwen2.5 only.
- Will the public preprint be revised, superseded or left standing? That is a human decision.
