## SUMMARY
The open manuscript (/Users/pat/code/ACE/paper/aistats_ace_2027/paper.tex, byte-identical to tag archive/delivery-scm-2026-11-02) is a TMLR-format, anonymous paper titled "From Experimental Histories to Causal
  Surrogates: Data Reuse, Optimization, and Error Propagation". Lines 1-3015 are a generated bundle of 16 embedded companion files (TMLR style, bst, bib, macro files, tables, theory). The narrative runs from line 3022 to
  4559. It assesses post-collection refitting ("delivery") of known-graph SCM surrogates on fixed histories. Its confirmatory core is mixed: delivery beats a flat refit in both families and beats rolling at 30 nodes, but
  is 2.46 times worse than rolling at 5 nodes, undiagnosed. It contains no LLM, DPO, acquisition policy or experimental-cost result. Acquisition appears only in four disclaimers. It has been on submission hold since
  November 2 as a supporting component study. Reusable for the broader paper: notation, three elementary propositions, the final-refit control arm, the registered-analysis template and negative controls. Every headline
  element is missing. Internal reviews flag narrow generality, tiny absolute errors, unequal compute, seed sensitivity and the unexplained reversal. No venue or deadline is committed for the broader paper.

## SURPRISES / CONTRADICTIONS
- paper/aistats_ace_2027/paper.pdf is a stale 30-page September build of the invalidated draft 'ACE: Language-Model Priors and Lookahead Selection for Active Causal Mechanism Estimation', not the current manuscript. The
  reviewed delivery PDF (26 pages) lives under /Users/pat/ACE_Study_Results and predates the archived source.
- paper/README.md is stale: it says 'Stages A/B/C remain pending' and gives AISTATS build notes. The November documents supersede it.
- October 24 declared preparation complete. November 1 called that 'too broad' (361.67 MB archive against a 100 MB limit). November 2 archived the paper.
- The handoff reports action collapse for the three LLM arms only. actions.csv shows the non-language ranker used just two actions, so the collapse is not specific to language.
- 'Target risk was best' holds for mean first-crossing cost and mean final NMSE. Menu coverage has the lowest geometric-mean final error (0.00721 vs 0.00849) and the best sustained-attainment cost. 'DPO was worst' also
  weakens: it ties the frozen LLM under sustained attainment.
- A coverage-style collection design lost to uniform random for both structured learners in all 20 five-node systems. The manuscript declines to interpret this.
- The agenda's proposed screen (16 + 64 budget, seven policies, four regimes of six worlds) differs from the executed one (8 + 24, eight policies, 24 single-system cells). The handoff says the executed protocol
  supersedes.
- The project's own pre-audit LLM draft already listed as its first contribution that DPO added nothing.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR AGENDA
- Do not propose repeating delivery studies A/B/C or editing the archived manuscript. Use it as a supporting section, a source of theory lemmas, and a predeclared final-refit arm crossed with intervention policies.
- Cheap strengthening of the supporting component uses saved artifacts: diagnose the five-node reversal from the 640 checkpoints (teacher-forced versus free-running error, off-support sensitivity), then add ancestral-
  root and compute-matched flat controls, seeds for every arm and a noisy variant.
- Redesign the policy endpoint before any LLM retraining. Ten of 24 systems start at target, first crossing rewards volatile curves, and rankings change under sustained attainment. Pre-register a sustained or area-under-
  curve endpoint with thresholds calibrated on training systems.
- Size the confirmation by power analysis. Paired SD is about 6 cost units, so a 1-unit gain over target risk needs roughly 280 systems. The agenda's 30-50 per family is likely underpowered without variance reduction.
- Run a headroom audit first. Target risk leads menu coverage by only 0.83 cost units. Propose regimes where a numerical planner is handicapped: misspecified mechanisms, large or compositional action menus, objectives
  and costs stated in prose, fallible semantic priors.
- Add the absent ablations: SCM-free and graph-removed arms, anonymized and misleading metadata, shuffled feedback, model size, compute-matched ranker. Diagnose state sensitivity for the numerical ranker as well as the
  LLM arms.
- Theory to add: a support-collapse result for constant-action policies built on Prop 8.3, the agenda's kappa + 2 epsilon + delta decomposition, and an exact linear-Gaussian design reference.
- Writing debt: rebuild related work, add data figures, re-verify every citation taken from older drafts, and never cite pre-audit LLM numbers or the stale paper.pdf.
- No deadline exists, so sequence work by evidence gates. TMLR's claims-and-evidence standard suits a scoped or diagnostic paper if an LLM benefit stays absent.
