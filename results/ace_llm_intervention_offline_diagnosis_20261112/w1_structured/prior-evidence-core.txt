# prior-evidence-core
## SUMMARY
No experiment in either repository shows an LLM proposer or policy beating a non-LLM control. The original ACE headline claims were invalidated by a four-confound metric audit: after matching the learner the 5-node
  result is a tie, and at 15 to 50 nodes ACE with DPO loses to Random. Mission-1 (pre-registered, 11 paired seeds, one emulator) gave an LLM versus random-proposer error ratio of 1.0412, inconclusive. My read-only
  analysis of its saved bundles shows the "LLM arm" was mostly hand-coded coverage fills plus the prompt's own few-shot example, so that study says the old pipeline's LLM added nothing but says little about what an LLM
  could add. The delivery (refit) results are solid learner effects, not acquisition evidence, and include an undiagnosed adverse 5-node prospective contrast. The CURC PEV ladder was never run. PEV was instead tested in
  a later persistent-campaign harness: it beat coverage on two synthetic families, failed its shifted-family primary gate, and never separated from naive variance. PEV has no code lineage to the November target-risk
  planner but is the same family of idea. LLM collapse onto one action is an eleven-month recurring pattern tied to prompt surface features, previously masked by heuristics. November is its first unscaffolded
  measurement.

## SURPRISES / CONTRADICTIONS
- Mission-1's LLM arm was 56-69% hand-coded fills and 9-13% the prompt's own example. Only a P2 code-health note hints at this; the study documents do not quantify it.
- The research pipeline has the same full-text parser fallback the agenda attributes to Runner (ace_experiments.py:796-802).
- The January 'single-value trap' DO X2=1.5 appeared one day after that example entered the prompt, and was diagnosed as learning dynamics. The link is my inference.
- X2 share sits just under the 70% hard cap in all five paper-era seeds, so the published 5-node strategy looks like a collapsed LLM plus the cap. Inference from counts and code.
- November constants coincide with menu positions: frozen model picks the first entry, trained models pick the second (first intervention). Menu order verified in llm_intervention_screen_protocol.py:67; the causal link
  is a hypothesis.
- Runner status (Oct 23) says ADR-0006's revisit trigger has not fired; the ACE ledger (Oct 24) reports the 5-node Stage B contrast failing its gate. No follow-up ADR exists.
- PEV confirmations are cited although the PEV ladder never ran. They come from a different harness (K=3, 20 epochs), so the ladder's pre-registered criteria, including PEV vs ACE at 5 nodes, were never tested.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR AGENDA
- Do not scale, retune or rerun DPO or the old pipeline. Diagnose state sensitivity first, and include prompt-surface permutations (menu order, action identifiers, examples, anonymized names) because prior constants
  tracked the first node, the few-shot example and possibly menu position.
- Every new LLM contrast needs a treatment-fidelity report before outcomes: per-candidate provenance (model output, fallback, fill), raw completion bound to parsed and executed action, and action entropy as a function of
  state.
- Establish headroom before testing any policy. Use task families where the best numerical planner clearly beats random and coverage and where initialization does not already meet the target. Mission-1 and the 10 of 24
  pre-solved November systems show how ties arise without it.
- Treat numerical SCM planning (target risk, PEV family, risk-pair selector) as both the bar and a defensible positive result. It is the only acquisition signal that has replicated, and CPU-only extensions across
  topologies and misspecification are cheap.
- Consider an LLM role on the prior side (mechanism-form elicitation) with rule-based and anonymized controls. It is the only place an LLM output helped (dev only), and the gain was form, not semantics.
- Never cite the 5-node win, pre-audit tables, Runner published win rates, old slide numbers, or the IVR formula. Cite delivery only as a learner effect and keep its adverse 5-node contrast visible.
- Keep the accepted statistical design: independent systems as units, frozen protocol, Holm, verdicts that include inferior and non-inferior, sample size from development variance (the Mission-1 LLM contrast had sd log
  0.175).
- Hold device, learner, evaluator and charged-call accounting identical across arms. Reserve fresh seed namespaces; many are consumed.
- Avoid experiments that depend on ace_experiments.py on GPU (unsolved host-RAM leak) or on Runner cloud images (registry deleted).
