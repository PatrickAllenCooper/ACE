# prior-evidence-portfolio
## SUMMARY
Since the September 2026 metric audit voided every original ACE-vs-baseline claim, the project has run five successive agendas. Every positive result is numerical or learner-side: delivery refit, SCM surrogate versus
  flat surrogate (dev), posterior-risk pair selection on two of three synthetic graph families, target-risk planning (best of eight in November), PEV-style model scoring on two of five ACE-Runner scenarios (dev), and
  cost-aware partial evaluation (dev, hypothetical costs). Every tested LLM role tied or lost to a data-only control: per-step proposing (original ACE, preregistered Mission-1, November DPO), mechanism-family proposals
  (BoxingGym Lotka, six invalid outputs on 9 October), and partial-identification reasoning. Semantic priors have one small supporting signal that vanished inside a strong learner. Action compilation from independent
  descriptions was never run with any model and is blocked on a human adjudicator. Translating varied objectives is untested. No external testbed yielded a usable adaptive task: CausalMan's probe menu was rejected,
  Causal Chambers is logged data that a two-coefficient law explains, NeuronBench rankings depend on the forecaster, and the compressor exists only as slides. ACE-Runner's five simulators are the only ready executable
  engineering environments. The set-transformer, memory, representation, hypothesis-search and perturbation-data packages were never started.

  governs, and the 10 November result matches the older warning.
- The Qwen3-0.6B model that failed the 9 October output contract became the November policy model after grammar constraints were added.
- A Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct snapshot is cached locally, yet no guidance document reports using it.
- The experiment registry is stale: A1a, C1a and B2a were executed on 2 October per the ledger.
- The first Causal Chambers physics control used a wrong formula; its scores are misspecified controls.
- The 25 September portfolio allowed 1,000 closed-model calls; every later document forbids paid APIs.
- The charter calls tlam_satellite unlearnable, while the later deck recreation reports a signal on it.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR AGENDA
- Do not propose: scaling the DPO recipe, same-generator PEV or pair-selection seed grids, threshold tuning on the 12 exposed fanout systems, Lotka or Signal expansions, direct-prompt partial identification on the
  20-task suite, Mission-1 root-design acquisition, the CausalMan probe menu, or an unconstrained JSON language channel.
- Any LLM-prior experiment needs a data-only selector over the same grammar, an anonymized placebo, misleading metadata, a rule-based bounded-output prior (not yet implemented) and a flexible non-pretrained control.
- Keep target-risk planning, coverage, endpoint coverage and the fixed factorial hub as controls; use exact enumeration wherever the action schema is formal.
- Choose tasks where the exact planner's assumptions fail: misspecified mechanism families, budgets too small for reliable selection, informal action descriptions. A known-graph linear-Gaussian menu leaves an LLM nothing
  to add.
- Acquisition headroom was only seen with intermediate or pair interventions on bottleneck graphs and with cost-aware partial evaluation. An LLM must beat the numerical allocator there.
- Judgment: cheapest concrete task is an ACE-Runner scenario (counter_uas, or tlam_mission1 with partial-evaluation costs) behind the November charged executor; Causal Chambers serves as a logged-data sanity row; the
  compressor requires a build.
- Action compilation needs an independent adjudicator before any model run.
- Test model scale once: all LLM evidence comes from models of 3B parameters or fewer.
