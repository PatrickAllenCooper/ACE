## SURPRISES / CONTRADICTIONS
- No numerical disagreement with the documents. But the ordering among weak arms flips with the endpoint: dpo minus frozen is +1.54 units at threshold 0.04 and -1.04 at 0.02.
- local_info beats TR on the informative 14 for log-area and for error at costs 16, 24 and 32.
- On the 17 systems where X=-1 is the high-leverage side, the constant x- policy matches TR on log-area.
- The per-system best policy is close to uniform: dpo has the lowest log-area on 4 systems, as many as TR.
- The eight true target means of every system are recoverable from saved predictions plus errors. Confirmation reports should stay sealed until methods are frozen.
- The executed budget (8 + 24) is far shorter than the agenda's design values (16 + 64). The handoff declares the agenda counts superseded.
- Summed per-query error decompositions are dominated by two systems per graph (w19 and w12 hold 98 percent of dpo's X-mean-dependent error in chains), so medians are reported alongside.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR AGENDA
- Do not run another 24-system single-run screen as evidence. It cannot detect any attainable improvement over the planner. Plan about 300 systems for a 1-unit claim, or use replicates.
- Before any new policy, recalibrate initialization and threshold on non-evaluation systems (threshold near 0.02 or fewer than 8 initial observations, at most about 10 percent at target after initialization) and replace
  the fixed scale of 2 by a public per-family scale.
- Add within-system replicates (about 8), common random numbers keyed by action occurrence instead of by arm, and a deliberate twin arm to measure replicate variance.
- Keep capped cost as primary and declare sustained cost and log-area as secondary. Curve-area endpoints should not be expected to add power.
- Superiority over the planner is close to unattainable in the linear-Gaussian known-noise family, where a static design is nearly optimal. Either target noninferiority at lower compute, or move to environments where
  adaptivity or semantic information matters.
- Add cheap decisive controls: always-x-, always-observe, the coverage cycle, a static far-side-X-then-observe heuristic and an oracle design. Keep both TR and local_info as references. The ranker is not a credible
  learned-numerical control.
- First state-sensitivity diagnostic: flip the sign of the posterior X mean in otherwise identical prompts and test whether set_x_neg and set_x_pos scores swap. Second: whether observe_all rises after two or three X
  interventions.
- Word paper claims carefully: 'planner best' and 'DPO worst' hold for the mean of one endpoint only.

## OPEN QUESTIONS
- What share of the paired variance is replicate noise for the strong policies? Only the constant x- policy has accidental twins (point estimates 0.47 for log-area, 0.81 for cost, wide ranges).
- Does any real headroom above TR survive replication in this family?
- Would a static 'far-side X, then alternate with observation' design match TR? This cannot be evaluated from saved data.
- Which initialization size and threshold leave at least 90 percent of systems informative across the three graphs?
- Does the near-side versus far-side split of the dpo deficit replicate on fresh systems? It rests on 7 against 17 systems and was found post hoc.

### ADDITIONAL FINDINGS (verifier)
- Target risk's first place on curve area comes from the 10 systems already at target. On the 14 informative systems local information leads (mean log-area -1.755 vs -1.624, lower on 9 of 14, sign-flip p 0.32). On the 10
  at-target systems local information is last of eight (+0.259 vs target risk, p 0.088). Neither is resolved.
- Tighter thresholds discriminate more, post hoc. At 0.02 target risk separates from random, coverage, ranker, frozen, supervised and DPO (p 0.0004 to 0.035) but not local information (0.20). At 0.01 all seven are below
  0.05, including coverage (+3.875, p 0.0007). Final NMSE and sustained cost separate nothing.
- Restricting to the 14 informative systems, the median paired SD is 7.53 and the equivalent of a 1-unit mixed-system difference needs 154 informative systems instead of 305 mixed ones. Excluding at-target systems by
  design roughly halves the number of systems.
- Replicate noise is policy dependent. The DPO/SFT twin difference variance at cap 18 (7.82) is 2.2 times the whole coverage-minus-target-risk variance, so the weak always-X policy is noisier than the strong ones.
  Replicate variance has not been measured for target risk itself.
- Target risk's action mix varies strongly between systems inside a graph-by-menu cell (chain/uniform observation share 0.25 to 0.71, Y share 0 to 0.58). Cell membership adds nothing to step prediction (label-shuffle p
  0.11). Step position adds a little (order-shuffle p 0.003). A constant 'always observe' guess matches local information as well as a per-cell fixed sequence (0.558 vs 0.570).
- The near-side split is not specific to DPO. The frozen LLM, which never intervenes, shows a similar deficit on the same 7 systems (+0.426 vs +0.096, p 0.055), and DPO minus frozen does not split (p 0.32). The pattern
  separates arms that never set X at the far extreme from arms that do. It is post hoc on 7 systems.
- On w02, w03 and w21 the initialized SCM predicts worse than the all-zero prior mean. The ranker's majority X choice matches the far extreme in only 10 of 24 systems.
- The noncentral-t power formula is roughly calibrated for realistic two-sided contrasts (bootstrap vs formula at n = 24: random 0.76 vs 0.62, coverage 0.23 vs 0.25, DPO 0.88 vs 0.73), so the 300 to 400 system estimates
  stand given SD near 6.2.
