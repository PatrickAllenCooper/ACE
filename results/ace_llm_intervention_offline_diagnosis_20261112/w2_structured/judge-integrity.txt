## MUST_INCLUDE
- Correct the public record before any new ACE result, talk or submission. Put a README banner on lines 14, 114 and 116 now; this is reversible and internal, but it needs the lead's go-ahead. The lead and the coauthor decide
  whether to replace or withdraw arXiv 2602.02451, after confirming it is this project's paper (identity currently rests on an identical title). Check the v9 slide deck. Every new manuscript gets a section relating it to the
  earlier public claims. Justification: any new paper will be read next to a 70-71% claim the project's own audit voided. Only paper-strategy times this correctly; policy-repair and advantage-theory are silent.
- Admit new classes by a rule that ignores LLM results, frozen and externally timestamped before any LLM is trained, prompted or scored in the class. Use numerical-only headroom against the strongest available references, show
  feasible n, include a zero-dose anchor, and declare a single primary class for the headline claim. Report every audited class, including failures and the completed three-node null, and allow at most two redesign rounds on LLM-
  independent criteria. Justification: this is what separates a theory-driven change of benchmark from a favorable replacement.
- Write a declared-handicap table for each class before its headroom audit. It names what the uninformed planner lacks and the numerical control that removes each handicap: an oracle-informed planner; a hand-written parser plus
  planner, built to a stated effort standard by someone not developing the LLM; a learned non-LLM text model (bag-of-words); a numerical student on frozen-LLM embeddings; a GO-CBED-style amortized policy with a training-budget
  curve; a rollout planner; and a compute-matched planner. Every learned arm gets the same data and the same tuning budget, on validation data only. Justification: this is the standing rule that numerical controls receive the same
  information.
- Make the confirmatory hypotheses co-primary. The LLM must beat both the strongest planner without the side information and the strongest numerical control that has the same information, and a full positive needs both. The
  confirmation must also contain: target risk; the numerical-student successor to the ranker; the frozen base LLM; the supervised-only LLM; text-removed, misleading-text, shuffled-feedback and state-permuted ablations; a control
  without language pretraining or using embeddings only; dose and placebo strata; and a leakage audit whenever a gain exceeds the computed ceiling. Justification: a win only over a planner that lacks the text shows that the text
  is valuable, which the audit already proves numerically. It does not show that the LLM contributes.
- Validate the instrument on fresh calibration systems with numerical arms only, before any LLM is scored. This means a check of CRN marginals, an A/A test, planted-effect power, synthetic policies with known information capture,
  a leakage canary policy that reads the private truth, and a calibration of expected against realized error. The primary endpoint must be unbiased for an adaptive policy: either ERB (the conditional expectation given the data
  actually observed, under the generating law) or realized truth error with replicates. Design quality with redrawn noise stays secondary unless this validation passes.
- Development discipline: training, validation, test and confirmation namespaces are disjoint and hashed. Use a fresh validation shard for each iteration, at most three iterations, and make every selection (recipe, lr, seed,
  escalation, DAgger round, model size) on validation data only. Score held-out test worlds once per frozen configuration. Record every attempt in a ledger and report the count in the paper.
- Behavioural acceptance gates, fixed in advance, must pass before any rollout or outcome scoring: legality; no single action dominating; agreement beyond a coarse-feature baseline; held-out cross-entropy below a state-blind
  counter; sensitivity to posterior edits with invariance to menu order and identifiers; and stability across seeds. Justification: the November collapse passed a legality-only gate. Because the thresholds are calibrated knowing
  the November failures, the paper may say the gates would have caught it but may not call that a validation.
- Fidelity-gated, forward-only forensics of the saved adapters must reproduce at least 99% of the saved decisions. Also check the base model's hash, because only the adapters are verified. Attribute the collapse interventionally
  with training-side factorial knockouts, using a numerical student on the same labels. Prompts from the development systems serve forensics only. Forensic outputs may change the method only through decision rules written before
  the forensics run, and any causal claim about the collapse needs interventional support.
- Pre-register with an external timestamp before any confirmation system is generated. The registration fixes: hashes of methods and adapters; namespaces; endpoints; failure treatment (failures scored at the cap, no system
  replaced, aborted hardware episodes counted in the primary analysis); hypotheses; n; a TOST equivalence margin for a null; the seed policy (all trained seeds confirmed, with recipe-level and instance-level claims separated); and
  the hash of the analysis code. Arm labels stay blinded, and a human analyst re-analyses independently before results are released.
- Controls against narration: no per-action planner statistics in the primary LLM input; a family of argmax-of-prompt rules; and a numerical student trained on exactly the prompt's features. Any adjudicator or re-ranker must
  deviate from argmax-of-prompt on a declared share of decisions, and those deviations must help on average. Hybrid and compiler roles are reported as narrowed claims that do not meet submission condition 1, unless the lead rules
  otherwise before any LLM result in the class.
- Account for responses and compute: synthetic training-world responses, relabeling draws and training and inference compute are reported separately from deployment-time experimental cost. The 'lower experimental cost' claim
  refers only to deployment responses, and the numerical planners' zero training cost is shown alongside.

## MISSING_FROM_ALL
- A mechanical boundary between private truth and policy code once numerical policies run in-process and runs move to the Mac, outside the custody harness: a separate process or API boundary, a hash of the public state at each
  decision, a leakage canary, and a provenance manifest (commit, environment hash, seeds) for every local run. Without it, in-process evaluation lets the policy and the private truth share memory.
- A named human independent reviewer who approves the evaluator, the registration and the freeze before any confirmation data are generated. Agents write the policy, the evaluator and the analysis, so a review by another agent is
  not the independent review that submission condition 5 requires.
- A claim hierarchy and attempt ledger for the whole program, covering every class, LLM role, model size, recipe and seed, and every interface choice driven by forensics, with the denominator reported in the paper. Holm correction
  within each experiment does not control a program that takes many shots at a positive result.
- A rule that venue deadlines (ICML on January 22) may cut scope but never change a registered n, endpoint, gate or failure treatment. A confirmation that cannot reach its registered n is reported as incomplete rather than run
  underpowered.
- Confirmation that arXiv 2602.02451 is this project's preprint, since the author list was never checked and identity rests on the title. Any new paper also needs a disclosure section covering the voided 70-71% claim, the hand-
  coded fills in Mission-1 and the eleven months of action collapse.
- A lead ruling, before any sign-up including the free-credit calibration, on whether the Remote Lab (a paid external service with a real-time API) is allowed at all under 'no paid APIs', and on its terms: jointly owned data that
  the company may publish, and marketing use of the customer's name.
- World-clustered inference for every state-level analysis, because states are nested in worlds and independent systems are the units. Also an equivalence margin for every test that can return a null; only evaluation-statistics
  (TOST) and experimentalist-task (a parity interval) have one.
- Retiring the 24 development systems and the 32 November training worlds from every evaluation role (only evaluation-statistics asks for this), and re-declaring for the new class the confirmation range [202611071000,
  202611072000), which was reserved under the old three-node protocol.

## RECOMMENDED SPINE
In week 0 the lead decides on the public record (a README banner now, and a preprint plan agreed with the coauthor after confirming the preprint is this project's), on an externally timestamped registration mechanism with a named
  human reviewer, and on the pending approvals; nothing new about ACE is presented until the README is corrected. Stage 1 runs three tracks in parallel, with no LLM training. The first validates the instrument and endpoints on
  fresh calibration systems with numerical arms: CRN, an A/A test, planted effects, synthetic capture policies, a leakage canary, and ERB against realized error. The second builds a headroom atlas over candidate classes with a
  declared-handicap table, ending in a hashed admission decision that names one primary class. The third runs fidelity-gated, forward-only forensics of the saved adapters together with a numerical learnability ladder on fresh
  training-namespace worlds. Stage 2 tests, in the completed three-node class on fresh namespaces and training-side data only, whether a repaired recipe can imitate the planner, with knockout attribution, numerical students on
  identical data and the mandatory behavioural gates; this settles submission condition 1 either way, feeds the diagnostic paper, and never claims an advantage in that class. Stage 3 develops the LLM in the admitted primary class
  with privileged-teacher training, a fresh validation shard per iteration (at most three), the same tuning budget for every learned arm, and the LLM's role and seed policy fixed by rules declared before any LLM result in that
  class. Stage 4 is a single registered confirmation on fresh systems: co-primary hypotheses against the uninformed planner and against the strongest numerical control given the same information, the controls the standing rules
  require, the ablation family, dose and placebo strata, a leakage audit whenever a gain exceeds the ceiling, all trained seeds, intention-to-treat failure scoring, a TOST margin, blinded analysis and a human re-analysis. Stage 5
  runs a transfer family registered with the confirmation (the wind-tunnel simulator, described as team-extended) and reports it whatever the outcome; hardware runs only after a lead ruling on paid services, with frozen policies,
  and is reported descriptively. Kill rules only narrow the claim: if no class is admitted, the paper becomes theory, atlas and diagnostics; if imitation fails, it becomes the diagnostic paper; a failed confirmation is reported as
  such, with no replacement class. Throughout, an attempt ledger counts every LLM run, recipe, seed and class, and simulated training responses and compute are reported separately from deployment cost. The delivery study and the
  methodology results proceed separately and do not depend on the LLM outcome. Venue deadlines may cut scope but never change a registered n, endpoint, gate or failure treatment.
