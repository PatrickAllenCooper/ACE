## MISSING_FROM_ALL
- Text as the statistical unit. Claims that the LLM reads language generalize over texts, not systems. No proposal models system x text crossed random effects, or the effective n given by a few phrasing families or templates, in
  its power analysis or confirmation.
- Winner's curse in class selection. Picking the cell with the largest headroom among many (about 60 in advantage-theory) inflates the gap. None re-estimates the selected class's headroom on fresh calibration systems before sizing
  the confirmation.
- Counterfactual-law systems that separate reading from recall, where text and simulator consistently describe a modified exponent or a flipped sign. Anonymization, fresh paraphrases and misleading text show whether text is used
  and whether it harms, not whether it is read rather than recalled from pretraining (the Causal Chambers documentation is public).
- A trained reasoning arm, or an explicit scope decision on reasoning tokens, for the transfer claim. Kriz et al. report that direct amortization without reasoning does not transfer robustly across domains, and Schmied et al. tie
  gains to chain of thought. Every proposal assumes no reasoning.
- Amortization and total-cost accounting. None says how training simulation, privileged-teacher labels and synthetic responses count against the 'lower experimental cost' headline, as against savings per deployment.
- A single hashed registration of the whole program decision tree (gate to claim to venue) with a global failure ledger across all development iterations and classes. Paper-strategy and evaluation-statistics each cover only part
  of this.
- World-clustered inference for state-level analyses (counterfactual sensitivity, probes, gate statistics). States from the same world are not independent, and several power calculations treat them as if they were.
- A predeclared non-inferiority (no-harm) fallback for the external transfer test if the wind-tunnel family has no admitted headroom. Condition (4) can then still be met with a correctly scoped claim, and transfer is not dropped.
