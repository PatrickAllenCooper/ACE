The next qualification trains an LLM adapter to reproduce and prefer interventions on the complete accepted artificial batch. It establishes that the integrated training and saved-checkpoint path works. It cannot establish that interventions are useful or cheap. The 32 training and 24 development descriptors remain ungenerated and unfrozen.

The worker loads the existing Qwen3-0.6B base with seed 109 and creates fresh rank 8, alpha 16 attention adapters. Failure never triggers a replacement record, alternate initialization or automatic retry.

Tokenization and record construction have finished on CPU. The only substantial CPU startup inside the GPU allocation is authentication and staging of the existing runtime into node-local scratch. The stage is performed once and shared by training and checkpoint inference in that job. The supervisor requires an owned NVIDIA compute process and positive CUDA work within 240 seconds from its original start. After that startup gate, it stops the owned worker if reliable GPU-process telemetry or advancing phase, update or inference records are absent for 90 seconds. This treats telemetry loss as uncertainty and a stalled phase as a failure to diagnose, rather than inferring idleness. It retains timestamps, process telemetry and completed update evidence. GPU utilization percentage remains unknown unless actually measured. Allocation or a successful exit alone is insufficient.
