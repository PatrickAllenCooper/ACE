# Bounded recommendation for the first fresh intervention screen

Independent read-only design analysis by Descartes on November 2, 2026. No worlds, models, tests or remote operations were executed. This is a proposed first screen, not a frozen scientific protocol or authorization to skip the later agenda.

## Recommendation

After runtime qualification, compare eight policies on the same 24 fresh development worlds: uniform random, coverage, local information, target risk, a learned numerical ranker, frozen LLM, supervised LLM and preference-trained LLM. Keep the learner, task, initial observations and evaluation law fixed. The question is whether training improves acquisition beyond frozen inference and numerical controls.

Reserve policy-training, development and confirmation namespaces before generation. A compact proposal uses 32 training worlds, then 24 development worlds. Four development regimes cross prior-matched versus shifted coefficients with low versus high known noise. In each regime, six worlds cross chain, fork and collider structures with uniform versus heterogeneous costs. The mechanisms remain stationary and linear. This does not test nonlinear-family or engineering transfer.

The proposed first-screen task uses three to five nodes, eight legal acquisition actions, eight equally weighted prediction queries, eight shared initial responses and 24 additional cost units. This bounds history length at 32 responses. It is a diagnostic screen under the full agenda, not a reduction of that agenda's confirmation and engineering requirements.

Collect training histories using an outcome-independent mixture of random, coverage, local-information and target-risk behavior. Fix acquisition-cost snapshot milestones. Assign supervised teacher policies before observing branch results. At each snapshot, use separately charged action branches and shared scoring responses. Four independent branch replicates per action are proposed to expose response-noise variation. Structural scoring responses are training-only privileged supervision and must be labeled and charged as such. They cannot enter deployment state or development feedback.

Both learned families must receive the same public states, supervised choices and strict preference labels. Keep ties and negative gains. Each preference stage uses its own fixed post-supervised reference. Terminal-budget states can teach stop. Negative one-step gain alone does not establish an optimal stopping label.

The proposed development accuracy threshold is normalized structural MSE at most0.04 under a fixed public scale. This is a synthetic diagnostic threshold, not an engineering requirement. Report private first-crossing cost, attainment, final error, full curves and actual spending together. Nonattainment and operational failures receive the common within-world cap for capped-cost reporting and remain separately counted. Private scores never control the policy's stopping decision. Worlds, not action choices or training pairs, are the paired statistical units.

## Required gaps before scientific execution

- Authenticate and separate the generator, public state and evaluator. Hashes alone do not prove honest public information, independent worlds or charged execution.
- Bind labels to the exact task objective. The existing branch helper averages scoring rows and handles one branch response per action. Its scoring-row standard error does not measure branch-outcome uncertainty. Replicate aggregation and equal query weights must be explicit in the new runner.
- Freeze a scientifically meaningful exposure and optimization schedule. The artificial eight-SFT/four-DPO-update smoke is not a training prescription. A failed tiny fit cannot justify a broad statement about LLM usefulness.
- Qualify context bounds, checkpoint reload, repeated deployment decisions, deadlines, cleanup and resources. Preserve failures without fallback, truncation, replacement worlds or free candidate outcomes.
- Keep the scientific scope clear. Development contrasts include DPO versus SFT, SFT versus frozen, DPO versus the numerical ranker and DPO versus target-risk acquisition. A random-only win is insufficient. Graph/feedback ablations, pretraining attribution, held-out families, independent environments, engineering outcomes and powered confirmation remain later requirements.

This recommendation is retained to guide the next implementation. Fresh scientific worlds remain ungenerated and no configuration in this document is retrospectively selected from outcomes.
