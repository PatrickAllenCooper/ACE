# Ranked panel revision disposition

Recommend a bounded textual revision that makes the existing evidence easier to interpret. Preserve the registered primary analysis and adverse results. Close explanatory gaps by narrowing language. New diagnostics, fits, and statistical tests are outside this revision sidecar.

All twelve model reports, the briefing, and the discussion note were read in full. Numerical values below are attributed to those sources, not independently recomputed here. The parent's numerical and source/budget verifiers should clear values, aggregation definitions, and implementation details before insertion. Section and table numbers refer to the reviewed version and may shift during revision. Checklist boxes indicate work for the parent, not completed manuscript edits.

**Evidence labels.** **F-AE** means a factual statement or recomputation reported in the supplied discussion note. **F-R** means a factual assertion in a model report that this sidecar did not independently verify. **F-local** means directly inspected local metadata. **J** means panel or synthesis judgment. **X** means an additional descriptive or inferential analysis, even when it uses already collected data. **P** means the supplied packet's policy account or a model's editorial prediction. A calculation can be both F-AE and X. Verification does not make an analysis preregistered.
