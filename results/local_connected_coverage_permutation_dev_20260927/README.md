# Connected-motif coverage permutation audit

This **post hoc development diagnostic** revisits the corrected connected-motif systems (seeds 200–202, N=30, root SD 0.15, actuator penalty 4, cost budget 400) without new systems. It enumerates all six motif orders at k=3 and samples 64 distinct, deterministic motif permutations per system at k=10, including the original identity order. Coverage policies use the new order; posterior-risk actions and all other conditions remain fixed. Each identity-order run reproduces every archived action and outcome exactly. The risk action ledger is identical across all permutations. The 1,050 metric rows, 7,350 action rows, 210 system/order summaries, and file hashes are in this directory.

At k=10, risk pairs have lower feasible-motif MSE than coverage pairs in **152/192 system×order scenarios** and lower than coverage singles in 158/192. By system, risk beats coverage pairs in 51/64, 63/64, and 38/64 orders. The risk MSEs are 0.05118, 0.02618, and 0.04895. Coverage-pair MSE minima across sampled orders are 0.01825, 0.01594, and 0.01551: some orders beat risk on *every* system. At k=3, risk wins 11/18 comparisons with coverage pairs. Thus the previous all-rotations k=10 finding did not extend to arbitrary permutations, and the k=3 weakness remains. The 192 scenarios share only three SCMs and must not be treated as independent replications or used for a p-value.

The result supports a narrower hypothesis: an adaptive risk selector may often improve on uninformed coverage order when the budget permits fewer paired actions than motifs, but its edge is contingent and could vanish against a strong order-independent or evidence-aware coverage policy. The existing connected-chain generator and exact linear posterior remain diagnostic tools; this is not evidence for a foundation-model contribution. No new CURC grid is justified. A future B experiment needs a distinct graph structure, a cost-matched adaptive coverage baseline, and fresh systems under a frozen primary comparison.

Reproduce with `python3 scripts/research/connected_coverage_permutation_dev.py --output results/local_connected_coverage_permutation_dev_20260927 --k10-orders 64`. The permutation RNG seed is fixed at `910000 + system seed`; the output protocol and receipt record it. No closed-source model API was used.
