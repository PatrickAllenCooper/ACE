# Post hoc held-out mechanism failure audit

This reads the frozen [connected transfer development result](../local_connected_transfer_heldout_dev_20260930/README.md) without adding training queries. Audit source revision `dc733562`; input source revision `76aaa3f6`. All 48 cell result hashes were checked. The diagnostic reconstructs the same sealed feasible-action contexts and fits the best linear three-feature projection to motif 7's true noise-free conditional mean. The projection is a retrospective lower bound for this learner class on that panel; it was inaccessible to the acquisition policies.

Across the 12 systems, motif 7's mean oracle-linear MSE is **0.12851**. Learned warm MSE is **0.33247** for risk pair at source16, **0.36668** for fixed pair at source16, **0.32072** for risk at source64, and **0.31577** for fixed at source64. The excess above the linear floor is therefore approximately **0.19–0.24** for warm arms. A richer basis could address part of the floor; the excess also requires better estimation or intervention-context coverage. The audit does not isolate those causes experimentally.

Each arm had 44 natural motif-7 labels, because its child was not overwritten in these schedules. Across 12 systems × five pair decisions, the risk policy directly targeted motif 7 only **three times** at source16 and **zero times** at source64; the fixed schedule targeted it zero times. Thus the target parent-context distribution, rather than raw label count alone, matters. Directly targeting motif 7 is not automatically optimal: a subsequent matched policy test would be needed.
