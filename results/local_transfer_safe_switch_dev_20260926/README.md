# Protected old-mechanism switch: development diagnostic

This local numerical screen uses the existing 12 development seeds (100–111), not fresh systems. `scripts/research/transfer_safe_switch_dev.py` reconstructs the exact v2 transfer data and checks its warm-start and Bayesian-mixture MSEs against all 72 archived development settings × three budgets × three metrics at absolute tolerance 1e−12 before scoring a new arm. The same fixed learned source library uses 2,560 separate training examples. Source hash, configuration, all 19,440 node-level outcomes, a 54-row summary, and hashes are saved here. Receipt/hash, finite-value, and unique-grid checks pass.

The diagnostic protects the old expert unless the marginal evidence for an equal-weight mixture of the four source experts exceeds the old expert by odds 4, 10, or 25. Once switched, it uses the source posterior mixture; otherwise it uses the old-centered posterior. The evidence test and fit use the **same acquired samples** at each budget, so this is not the planned prequential candidate/confirmation switch. No hidden changed-node label or held-out test error enters the switch decision.

All three prespecified odds thresholds meet the written *development* gates at budgets 120 and 200. At odds 10 and 200 samples, changed-node candidate/warm MSE ratios for family changes are 0.246, 0.147, and 0.264 at k=1,3,10. For coefficient changes they are 1.000, 1.000, and 1.000. Untouched-node ratios are 1.000 in every condition at 120 and 200, with zero false switches on these development systems. At 120, family changed-node ratios are 0.612, 0.284, and 0.468; coefficient k=10 is 1.023. See `summary.csv` for all budgets, odds, and switch rates.

The near-perfect separation is a warning about this favorable synthetic feature-bank generator: it may make family changes easy to identify and does not establish safe transfer under unmodeled forms, upstream distribution shift, or noisy sparse observations. Odds 10 is a conservative candidate for the next *development* check, but no fresh confirmation is justified until a counted prequential validation step and a misspecified-family negative control are implemented and pass. Do not use this in-sample diagnostic as an independent result or allocate a neural hypernetwork/GPU run on its basis.
