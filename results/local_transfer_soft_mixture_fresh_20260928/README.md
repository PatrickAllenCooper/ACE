# Fresh-system finite-source soft mixture (28 September 2026)

The [protocol](../../docs/development/guidance/protocol_transfer_soft_mixture_fresh_2026-09-28.json) was committed at `a6d9740` before systems 700–719 were generated. Code revision `ce2f137` (full hash in `suite_complete.json`) fixed a source-versus-scratch Bayesian model average with a prior change probability of 0.1. No change labels or latent coefficients enter the policy; they are used only in evaluation. All arms receive identical 200-response uniform target prefixes. The two source-size arms use 16 or 64 separately acquired source responses per node.

The **prespecified point gate fails in four of 24 strata**. Changed-node MSE ratios for the mixture versus the specified control at k=1 are 1.207 (16 source examples) and 1.299 (64) for family changes, where scratch is the control. For coefficient changes they are 1.276 and 1.067 against the better aggregate source-warm or scratch control. Each had to be at most 1.05. The k=3/10 strata pass the point gate. Across all settings, untouched-node ratios versus source-warm lie between 0.9999 and 1.0004, so the failure is sparse changed-node recovery rather than damage to the majority of nodes.

Paired 20-system bootstrap intervals and per-seed means are in `analysis/strata.csv` and `analysis/per_seed.csv`; 10,000 bootstrap resamples use seed 20260928. For family k=1, 95% percentile intervals for the ratio are [1.000, 1.821] and [1.000, 2.237] at source sizes 16 and 64. The uncertainty is substantial, and the frozen point criterion still fails. Selection of the better coefficient-change control from this same data makes those particular comparisons descriptive, not independent tests.

Custody: 40 source cells contain **48,000 counted source training responses** across both source-size arms. The 240 target setting cells contain **48,000 counted target responses**. Every source/target receipt, stored data/metric hash, source identity, finite score, exact per-method target count, and deterministic replay was checked. The analysis has 24 strata and per-seed records; its replay is byte-identical. All 20 systems were new to this transfer series, but the model family and feature basis match the generator. This is a prospective synthetic validation of this one frozen rule, not evidence that a foundation-model architecture works or that the method generalizes under misspecification. No CURC job or closed-model API call was needed for this short local numerical run.

The method is not promoted. The remaining bottleneck is k=1 change detection/recovery at six or seven target responses per node. Subsequent work should test a different information allocation or a held-out mechanism family under its own protocol, without retuning this mixture on these 20 systems.
