# Audit of missed finite-source switches

This evaluation-only audit reads the 144 validated cells of the [finite-source switch screen](../local_transfer_finite_source_switch_dev_v1_clean_20260928/README.md) at the 200-response target budget. It generates no new target or source observation and does not evaluate a new policy. `complete.json` contains the source revision and SHA256 of every input cell; a second run produced byte-identical summary and receipt files.

Across family-change settings, with **16 source examples per node**, 25 of 168 changed-node cases remain unswitched: 11 fail the four-observation nomination and 14 fail confirmation. All 11 non-nominated cases have later scratch-versus-source evidence above the frozen log(4) threshold, but the existing rule cannot revisit nomination. The 25 missed cases account for a summed MSE excess of 29.604 over scratch on the same acquired target observations. With **64 source examples**, 14 of 168 remain unswitched: three fail nomination and 11 fail confirmation; all three non-nominated cases likewise have later evidence above log(4). Their summed excess is 20.395.

Dropping nomination is not a validated fix. Among the **912 unchanged family nodes** per source-size arm, later evidence alone exceeds log(4) for six nodes at source size 16 and three at 64. Switching those nodes to scratch would add 2.397 and 0.539 summed MSE, respectively, relative to their warm-source fits. Including coefficient-change settings, the counts are 12/1,824 and 7/1,824 unchanged nodes. These post hoc counterfactuals identify the tradeoff; they are not a new controller result.

The next policy should have a prespecified way to reconsider an early non-nomination while controlling false switches. Its test must use untouched systems and a separately frozen rule. The current development seeds have already informed this diagnosis and cannot be treated as independent confirmation. No CURC job or closed-model API call was made.
