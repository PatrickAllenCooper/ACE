# Fresh endpoint-coverage replication on shifted mechanisms

The [frozen protocol](../../docs/development/guidance/protocol_pev_endpoint_replication_2026-09-27.json) compared graph-aware endpoint coverage with PEV and the naive-variance PEV variant on **20 fresh shift30 systems**, seeds 6000–6019. These systems use the same hierarchical graph generator and shifted function-family generator as prior work, though each has a distinct drawn DAG. This is within-generator replication, not an independent external-domain test.

All policies used the same persistent ensemble learner and exactly 2,000 environment samples over 32 executed actions. The prespecified outcome was final, post-update, noise-free feasible nonroot MSE; lower is better. Mean MSE was **0.019032 for endpoint coverage**, **0.015347 for PEV**, and **0.017491 for naive-variance PEV**. Endpoint coverage minus PEV averaged **+0.003685**, with paired 95% t interval **[+0.000452, +0.006917]**, two-sided paired t p=0.0276, and endpoint coverage lower on 7/20 systems. The primary hypothesis that endpoint coverage is better failed; the estimated direction favors PEV in this generator. Endpoint coverage minus naive variance averaged +0.001541, interval [−0.000872, +0.003954], p=0.197; this secondary comparison is unresolved. All 20 paired differences are in `comparison_receipt.json` and the per-system outcomes in `comparison.csv`. No best checkpoint or seed selection was used.

Endpoint coverage used only absolute intervention value 5 and visited a mean 19.6 unique targets per system. PEV used mean absolute value 4.539 and visited 16.25 targets; naive variance used 4.780 and 17.5. These action logs do not identify which aspect of PEV caused its edge. The three reused development systems had favored endpoint coverage, so their outcome did not generalize to this fresh sample. The earlier shift30 study had a borderline PEV-versus-ordinary-coverage primary result (p=0.051) and PEV was tied with naive variance. Combined interpretation: PEV is competitive with strong value and coverage controls here, but there is no basis to claim the integrated-variance formula itself adds value or that this carries to another graph generator or external domain.
