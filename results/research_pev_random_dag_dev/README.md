# Sparse random-DAG graph-shift development pilot

This nine-cell development pilot tested whether the PEV comparison survives a graph-generator change while retaining the same 30-node shifted mechanism family, learner, exact 2,000-sample budget, and final noise-free feasible nonroot MSE. The [protocol](../../docs/development/guidance/protocol_pev_random_dag_dev_2026-09-27.json) was frozen before submission. Each system has five roots and one or two parents per other node, chosen from all earlier nodes rather than constrained to adjacent layers. Seeds 42, 123, and 456 produced 34, 42, and 37 edges, including 15, 20, and 10 edges spanning more than ten node positions, respectively.

Final MSE means over the three systems: endpoint coverage **0.014648**, PEV **0.013687**, and naive-variance PEV **0.015965**. PEV was lower than endpoint coverage on seeds 42 and 456 but higher on seed 123. Its mean advantage is small (−0.000961). Naive variance was higher than endpoint coverage on seeds 42 and 123, and slightly lower on seed 456. The prespecified development gate required consistently lower outcomes without a severe failure before launching fresh confirmation; **no arm clears that gate**, so no 20-system graph-shift grid is submitted from this pilot. Three systems cannot establish equivalence or superiority. `comparison.csv` records per-system values and action magnitudes.
